The problem

Booking a holiday home still means trusting a stranger.

In most beach and city destinations across the region, short stays are arranged in chat groups and through brokers. A deposit goes out on a promise.

Informal supply

Word of mouth and brokers

Owners rent through messaging groups. Availability is a phone call, and a deposit is a leap of faith.

Opaque pricing

A different number for every caller

Prices move with the season, the caller's accent and the broker's mood. Guests never know if they paid the right price.

No recourse

Nobody to call

No verified identity, no booking record, no one to call when the key does not turn up. One bad stay and the guest never books again.
