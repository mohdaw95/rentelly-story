The problem

Booking a holiday home still means trusting a stranger.

In most of the region's beach and city destinations, short stays are arranged in chat groups and through brokers. A deposit goes out on a promise. Sometimes the home is not what the photos showed. Sometimes nobody answers.

Informal supply

Owners rent through word of mouth, brokers and messaging groups. Availability is a phone call, and a deposit is a leap of faith.

Opaque pricing

Prices move with the season, the caller's accent and the broker's mood. Guests never know if they paid the right number.

No recourse

No verified identity, no booking record, no one to call when the key does not turn up. One bad stay and the guest never books again.

The idea

Verified homes. Clear prices. A booking someone stands behind.

Rentelly takes the informal rental market and gives it the three things it never had: a checked listing, a transparent nightly price, and a protected reservation. The home is still the owner's. The trust is ours.

Why guests come back

Trust is the product. Everything else is a feature.

Verified listings

Every home is checked before it goes live. Owners are identified, photos match the property, and the listing says what the stay actually includes.

Transparent pricing

One nightly price in local currency, shown up front. No seasonal surprises, no different numbers for different callers.

Protected booking

Reserve with Rentelly and there is a record on both sides, a confirmation, and a team to call if something goes wrong.

Bilingual, local support

Arabic and English across the app and the web, with a support line that answers around the clock in the guest's own language.

Hosts win too

Free listing, a calendar instead of a phone that never stops ringing, and guests who arrive already trusting the booking.
